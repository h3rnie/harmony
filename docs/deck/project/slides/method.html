The method

One unit, every satellite, weighted by what it can see

01

Filter

Keep confident vegetation fires. Mask gas flares and other static heat sources.

02

Count cell-days

A 1.4 km cell that burned on a given day or night counts once, whatever the pixel size.

03

Calibrate

VIIRS against MODIS over their shared years, by biome, region, day or night and month.

04

Correct the drift

MODIS is corrected for its shifting overpass time, measured against VIIRS.

05

Fuse

Every satellite flying contributes, weighted by its measured error, with a stated range.

record = w · MODIS (drift-corrected) + (1 − w) · k · VIIRS

The weight w comes from each sensor’s measured error. Unit: AFCD-M, MODIS-equivalent active-fire cell-days, built only from NASA FIRMS data.

Harmony · NASA Space Apps 2026

3 / 7

Most approaches fit a ratio in the overlap years and switch from one sensor to the other. Harmony goes further in three ways. First, the unit: a cell that burned on a given day or night counts once, which removes most of the pixel-size gap. Second, the calibration is fitted by biome, region, time of day and month. Third, and this is the difference: there is no switch. Each sensor’s error is estimated from the data by triple collocation, MODIS is corrected for the drift of its own orbit, and every month is a weighted combination of all the satellites flying, with a range calibrated on held-out years. When MODIS and Suomi NPP retire, the record simply continues on what is left.
