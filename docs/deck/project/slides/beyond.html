Beyond what the challenge asks: the Global pulse plays every month since November 2000 on a globe rendered with WebGPU, and any point on it is a way into the analysis. This frame is September 2019. A live layer shows what satellites detected in the last hours, through a server-side proxy so the NASA key never reaches the browser, and reads it as a pace against the normal for this month. An AI brief turns the statistics into a paragraph a duty officer can read aloud, and you can ask follow-up questions; it answers only from the numbers on the page. And the method lab runs the neural cross-check live.
