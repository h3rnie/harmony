Harmony exists because of open data from space agencies. For 26 years NASA’s MODIS instruments on Terra and Aqua, and then the VIIRS instruments on Suomi NPP, NOAA-20 and NOAA-21, flown by NASA and NOAA, have detected active fires worldwide. NASA’s FIRMS service publishes every detection openly. Harmony reads all of it: 658 million detections, of which 536 million pass its filters, from the standard-quality archive and the near-real-time feed. Nothing else goes in. But the record is at a hand-over. Suomi NPP stopped delivering data on 2 November 2026, and MODIS is close to retirement.
