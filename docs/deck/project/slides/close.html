Harmony is for three groups. Responders get a plain answer to “is this unusual?”, in the same unit as the last 26 years, with a live reading. Scientists get a documented, validated series with its uncertainty and its limits stated, and the code is public. Land managers get the rhythm of a place: when it burns, how that is shifting, which years to learn from. It is built entirely on NASA FIRMS data, more than 650 million detections from MODIS and VIIRS. We acknowledge the use of data and imagery from NASA’s Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS), part of NASA’s Earth Observing System Data and Information System (EOSDIS). The app is live at harmony.hernie.me and the source is at github.com/h3rnie/harmony. Next steps are cropland-specific calibration and a cross-check against burned area. This work is dedicated to everyone who works the fire line. I am Toh Yu Heng, from Singapore. Thank you.
