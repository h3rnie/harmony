Harmony is for three groups. Responders get a plain answer to “is this unusual?”, in the same unit as the last 26 years, with a live reading. Scientists get a documented, validated series with its uncertainty and its limits stated. Land managers get the rhythm of a place: when it burns, how that is shifting, which years to learn from. It is built entirely on NASA FIRMS data, more than 650 million detections from MODIS and VIIRS. We acknowledge the use of data from NASA’s Fire Information for Resource Management System, part of EOSDIS. Next steps are cropland-specific calibration, a cross-check against burned area, and a public deployment. This work is dedicated to everyone who works the fire line. Thank you.
